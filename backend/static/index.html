Questions people ask first

What happens when the trial ends?

Live updates stop. The dashboard still opens, frozen on the market as it looked when you opened it. Once your account is extended it goes live again within a few seconds, without signing in again.

Can I use one account on two devices?

One at a time. Signing in on a new device signs the old one out automatically, and a second tab takes over from the first. That rule is part of why the price can stay this low.

Which markets does it cover?

The Binance BTCUSDT perpetual by default, and the Binance BTCUSD coin-margined perpetual. Trades, open interest, funding and liquidations also come in from Bybit, OKX, Binance spot and Coinbase, and the gamma levels come from Deribit options. No altcoins, no stocks.

Is it really live?

Yes. The data comes from the exchanges' public feeds: the order book every 100 ms and every trade as it prints. The typical delay from Binance to our server is 64 ms; your own internet adds a little on top.

How do I pay?

Press Pay in the dashboard. Razorpay's secure window opens, and you can pay by UPI, card or net banking. Your account is extended by 30 days the moment the payment goes through, with no waiting. You can pay early too: the days are added to the time you have left.

Will it tell me what to trade?

No. Flowdeck shows market data and a few model-based levels, like gamma walls and absorption. It's a way to see what other traders are doing, not a signal service, and nothing here is financial advice.
